Not every grid is feeling it

Baseload here is the 5th-percentile hour of each month: the demand that is there at 4 a.m. in mild weather. Round-the-clock loads such as data centers raise it directly, and weather moves it less than it moves peaks.

Filled markers are changes that are statistically significant at the 5% level, using Newey-West standard errors. Other things also raise baseload, including new factories, crypto mining and electrification.
