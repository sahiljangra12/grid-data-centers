Dating the change matters. A break that lines up with a known event is evidence about cause; one that does not is a warning.

When the bend came

Each zone’s share of total PJM demand cancels out most weather, because a heat wave lifts every zone at once. A sup-F test searches every possible month for a change in the trend’s slope; a block bootstrap gives the p-value.
