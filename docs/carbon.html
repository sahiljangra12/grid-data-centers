What the extra demand emits

New load is not served by the grid’s average mix. It is served by whichever plants turn up when demand rises.

The next megawatt-hour is dirtier than the average one

Nuclear plants run flat out regardless of demand. When demand rises, the plants that respond are mostly gas and coal. The marginal emission factor captures this: it is the slope of hourly changes in CO₂ on hourly changes in demand, holding wind and solar output fixed.

Carbon accounts that multiply new load by the grid’s average intensity miss that difference.
