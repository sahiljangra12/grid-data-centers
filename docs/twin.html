Each zone against its twin

A synthetic control answers a counterfactual question: what would demand in this zone look like if it had kept moving with its neighbors?

Measured against a statistical twin

A synthetic control builds a stand-in for each zone: a weighted mix of other PJM zones, with weights chosen so the mix reproduces the zone’s demand before 2023. The same weather systems and economy move all of them, so what is left after 2023 is what is particular to the zone.

To judge whether a gap is unusual, the same procedure is run on every other zone as if it were the one under study. The grey band shows where 90% of those placebo gaps fall.

Could it be chance?

Run the same method on a zone where nothing special happened and it will still show some gap after 2023. The test statistic is the size of a zone’s error after 2023 relative to its error before. With comparison zones, a zone that has the largest ratio of all gets a p-value of , which is the smallest this design can produce.

All specifications

Leave-one-out drops each comparison zone with real weight and refits. The placebo start date uses only pre-2023 data and pretends the build-out began two years early; a gap there means the zone was already diverging.
